Source: SEVP / university ISSS offices — Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is off-campus employment authorization for F-1 students that is an integral part of an established curriculum (e.g., a required internship, co-op, or practicum tied directly to a course for credit).

Key points:
- CPT must be authorized by the school's Designated School Official (DSO) BEFORE the student begins work — there is no USCIS application involved, unlike OPT.
- CPT is typically tied to enrollment in a specific course requiring the practical training component.
- CPT can be part-time (20 hours/week or less) or full-time (more than 20 hours/week).
- Full-time CPT used for 12 months or more at the same degree level eliminates eligibility for OPT at that same degree level. Part-time CPT does not affect OPT eligibility.
- Unlike OPT, CPT does not require a separate EAD card — authorization is reflected directly on the I-20.
- A student must have been enrolled full-time for one full academic year before becoming eligible for CPT (with limited exceptions for some graduate programs where the internship is required immediately).
