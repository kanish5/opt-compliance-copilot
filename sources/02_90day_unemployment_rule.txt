Source: USCIS / university ISSS offices — Post-Completion OPT Unemployment Limit

Once post-completion OPT is approved, a student cannot exceed a total of 90 days of unemployment, counted cumulatively, beginning with the EAD start date.

This unemployment limit does NOT apply to students on pre-completion OPT who are maintaining full-time enrollment and still pursuing their studies — it applies only to post-completion OPT.

Exceeding the 90-day unemployment limit is considered a failure to maintain F-1 status and can result in the termination of the student's SEVIS record and loss of lawful status in the United States.

Students should track unemployment days carefully starting from their EAD start date. Time spent unemployed while searching for a job, between jobs, or after a job ends all counts toward this 90-day limit unless later approved for the STEM OPT 24-month extension, which carries its own separate unemployment allowance.
