Source: USCIS / DHS / university ISSS offices — 24-Month STEM OPT Extension

The STEM OPT extension allows eligible F-1 students who completed a degree in a STEM-designated field to extend their post-completion OPT by 24 months, in addition to the initial 12 months of standard OPT (36 months total).

Eligibility — all of the following must be met:
1. Currently in an active period of approved post-completion OPT.
2. Earned a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree in a field on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List (verify via the CIP code on page 2 of your I-20), within the last 10 years, from an SEVP-certified school.
3. Have a paid job or job offer directly related to the STEM field of study, working a minimum of 20 hours per week.
4. The employer must be enrolled in E-Verify and have a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN).
5. Self-employment, volunteering, and unpaid work are NOT permitted on the STEM OPT extension.

Application timing:
- Students must apply for the STEM OPT extension BEFORE their initial 12 months of standard OPT expires.
- Applications may be submitted as early as 90 days before the initial OPT end date.
- If filed within the required window, the student may continue working for up to 180 days while the STEM extension application is pending with USCIS, even if the initial EAD has expired.

Required document: Form I-983 (Training Plan for STEM OPT Students), completed jointly by the student and employer, describing the formal training plan.

Reporting requirements during the 24-month STEM extension (failure to comply can result in loss of status and work authorization):
- Every 6 months: submit a validation report confirming current employer, address, and employment information (due within 10 days of each 6-month interval from the STEM OPT start date — i.e., at 6, 12, 18, and 24 months).
- At 12 months: submit a self-evaluation on the I-983 (page 5), signed by both student and employer.
- At 24 months (end of extension): submit a final evaluation on the I-983.
- Any change of employer, job title, or employer address must be reported within 10 days.
- Employers must notify the school's international office within 48 hours of any termination of employment.

Unemployment limit during STEM OPT extension: students may not be unemployed for an aggregate of more than 150 days (this is separate from, and in addition to, the 90-day limit during the initial 12-month OPT period — the two periods are not combined).

DHS/ICE may conduct random site visits to employers to confirm the training plan is being followed.
