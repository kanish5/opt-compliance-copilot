Source: USCIS / university ISSS offices — Optional Practical Training (OPT) Overview

Optional Practical Training (OPT) is temporary employment authorization directly related to an F-1 student's major field of study. During OPT, a student remains in F-1 status. Students request this employment authorization through U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS).

Eligibility:
- Must have been a full-time student for at least one full academic year (typically 3 terms) at an SEVP-certified school.
- Must currently be maintaining valid F-1 status and be in good academic standing.
- Must not have used more than 12 months of OPT at the current degree level.
- A job offer is NOT required to apply for standard post-completion OPT.

Types of OPT:
- Pre-Completion OPT: used before completing the academic program, limited to 20 hours/week while school is in session.
- Post-Completion OPT: used after completing the academic program, up to 12 months.

Application window:
- Post-completion OPT applications may be submitted to USCIS from 90 days before to 60 days after the program completion date.
- The requested OPT start date cannot be more than 60 days after graduation, and cannot be changed once the application has been mailed/submitted.
- USCIS must receive the I-765 application within 30 days of the date the OPT recommendation was entered into SEVIS by the school's Designated School Official (DSO), and before the 60-day post-completion grace period expires — whichever comes first. Late applications are denied.

Process:
1. Request an OPT recommendation and new I-20 from your school's international student office (ISSS/DSO).
2. File Form I-765 with USCIS online or by mail, along with the filing fee (varies; check the current USCIS fee schedule), a copy of the OPT-recommended I-20, I-94 record, passport/visa copies, and photos if filing by mail.
3. Once approved, USCIS issues an Employment Authorization Document (EAD / Form I-766).
4. The EAD card must be in-hand and its start date must have arrived before beginning any employment — paid, unpaid, or volunteer.

Normal USCIS processing time is typically 60-90 days, though this can vary.
